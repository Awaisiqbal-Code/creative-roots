About Creative Roots

Pakistan's premier enterprise partner for corporate branding, bespoke promotional gifts, precision offset packaging, and grand exhibition fabrication.

Ch. Adnan Ghani

Founder & Chief Executive Officer — Creative Roots

Ch. Adnan Ghani is the Chief Executive Officer of Creative Roots and Founder of Pakistan Corporate Branding Network. With an extensive background in Industrial Design, Supply Chain Strategy, and Corporate Brand Activation, he brings a modern commercial vision to Pakistan's physical marketing sector, seeking to revolutionize corporate gifting, bespoke packaging, and enterprise event infrastructure.

Having spearheaded high-impact branding operations across Lahore, Islamabad, Karachi, and nationwide manufacturing hubs, he leads corporate partnerships across banking, healthcare, education, real estate, and consumer brands. As a staunch advocate of indigenous craftsmanship combined with European-standard offset precision, he has pioneered turnkey solutions that bridge digital brand design with flawless physical execution.

Under his stewardship, Creative Roots has grown from a specialized Lahore fabrication studio into a trusted nationwide authority trusted by Fortune-level corporations, prestigious academic institutions, and leading industrial developers.

Who We Are

We started off as a small, specialized corporate printing business in Lahore. Now we have grown into one of the most trusted physical branding and corporate fabrication solutions providers in Pakistan. We realized that there were many gaps in the market: organizations were brimming with remarkable marketing ideas, but had no reliable single-source partner to vent them into reality. This is where Creative Roots stepped in.

Our Story

From bespoke corporate welcome boxes and exhibition pavilions to nationwide brand activations, Creative Roots provides an uncompromising commitment to quality. Our central production facilities integrate precision Heidelberg offset presses, Japanese UV-curing systems, high-speed fiber laser marking, and dedicated rigid box artisans to deliver enterprise-grade finishes on time, every time.
