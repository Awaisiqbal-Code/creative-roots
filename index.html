Contact Specialists
What is your delivery time for corporate bulk orders?

Standard delivery across Lahore, Islamabad, and Karachi is 4 to 6 business days following artwork confirmation. We also offer 24-48 hour expedited delivery for urgent corporate summit and exhibition deadlines.

How do I pay online for my corporate order?

We support Direct Corporate Bank Transfers (IBFT), Cross Cheques, Online Invoicing via PayFast/Debit/Credit Cards, and official Purchase Orders (PO) for pre-approved corporate clients.

Can I track my corporate order in real time?

Yes! Once your order goes into production, you will be assigned a dedicated project manager who provides visual progress shots and courier tracking numbers right up to delivery.

Do you offer nationwide and international shipping?

Yes, we ship to all major cities across Pakistan (Lahore, Karachi, Islamabad, Faisalabad, Multan, Peshawar, Sialkot, Quetta) and facilitate international corporate dispatches upon request.
